## Third-party assets that are NOT included

These are optional. If you want to use them, download them yourself from the original
author and **check the license shown on their page** before publishing your project.

- **Glass Flowers 3D Assets** by Spacekayak — <https://spacekayak.xyz> (Figma Community).
  Put the exported files in `src/assets/flowers/mono/` and `src/assets/flowers/color/`.
- **illus – Vector illustrations** — <https://illus.framer.wiki>. No license is stated on the site.
  Put the files in `src/assets/illus/`, named after the `icon` key of each stage.
- Your own **photos** (`src/assets/photos/`) and **music** (`src/assets/music/`).
  Only use music you have the rights to share.

Without those files the project falls back to the original, code-generated placeholders.
